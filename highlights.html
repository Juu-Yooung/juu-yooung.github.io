Awards Across All Three 2026 KSME-Linked Competitions

KIMM Cyber Lab · KSME–LG · KSME–SEMES

I received awards in all three 2026 competitions linked to the Korean Society of Mechanical Engineers (KSME): the Excellence Award (최우수상) at the 5th KIMM Cyber Lab Competition, the Bronze Award (동상) at the 5th KSME–LG Future Home Tech Challenge, and the Encouragement Award (장려상) at the 11th KSME–SEMES Open Innovation Challenge.

AwardNov 12, 2026

5th KSME–LG Future Home Tech Challenge · Bronze Award

Team KAIST MNIL · KSME & LG Electronics

Our KAIST MNIL team received the Bronze Award (동상) for a resonant mass-sensing concept for real-time metal corrosion monitoring and remaining-life prediction. The approach uses a hollow tube as the resonant sensor and multiple vibration modes to track corrosion-related changes in mass and stiffness, with potential applications in appliances and industrial equipment.

AwardOct 21, 2026

5th KIMM Cyber Lab Competition · Excellence Award

Korea Institute of Machinery and Materials

I received the Excellence Award (최우수상) for virtually modeling MEMS resonators to decouple temperature and mass cross-sensitivity. Using KIMM-CAD and KIMM-Structure, the project combined geometry modeling, coupled thermo-mechanical vibration analysis, mode tracking, and design comparison, with cross-validation against commercial simulation software.

Tech Xplore

Tech Xplore featured our research at KRISS on a waveguide-based ultrasonic sensor for inspecting structures in hazardous environments. The design sends ultrasonic waves in all directions while keeping the sensing element away from the inspection surface.
